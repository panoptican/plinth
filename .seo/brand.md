# PLINTH brand and voice

## Positioning

**Inferred from the repository; unconfirmed.**

PLINTH is an independent literary journal archive for readers of experimental,
speculative, and boundary-pushing writing who want to encounter work outside a
mainstream canon. It wins through seven preserved issues with intentionally
distinct art direction and a browsable record of an alternate canon. It is not a
commercial publishing service, a general book-discovery site, or a confirmed
current submissions portal.

## Audience

- Readers looking for experimental poetry, prose, weird fiction, and hybrid work.
- Writers, editors, and students researching independent literary journals and
  small-press literary history.

## Voice

Experimental, literary, concise, strange, art-directed, anti-canon, archival.
The existing copy favors fragments and evocative claims over explanatory
marketing language. Preserve each issue's visual and editorial character.

Avoid unsupported superlatives such as “best,” “definitive,” or “the leading.”
Do not imply that the archive accepts current submissions unless the site says
so explicitly.

## Evidence

- `README.md`: describes a seven-issue static literary journal archive spanning
  2013–2017.
- `src/index.html`: describes PLINTH as collecting and manifesting an alternate
  canon.
- `src/issue01/` through `src/issue07/`: issue-specific pages, assets, and
  distinct art direction.
